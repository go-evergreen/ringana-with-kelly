About Me · Wellness. Motherhood. Purpose.

From personal transformation to purpose.

I'm a wife of 18 years, mom of five, foster mom, wellness educator, and entrepreneur — and my heart is helping families create healthier homes through intentional choices.

The Short Version

Hi, I'm Kelly.

I'm a wife of 18 years, mom of five, foster mom, wellness educator, entrepreneur, graphic designer, and author of two ebooks. More than anything, I'm a mom who understands how overwhelming it can feel to navigate all the information about health, ingredients, and the products we bring into our homes.

When I became a first-time mom in 2009, I honestly had no idea what toxins were or how much our everyday choices could impact our health. Like many families, I trusted the products on store shelves without questioning the ingredients or the companies behind them.

After a lingering illness following the birth of my second child, my search for answers began a journey that completely changed the way my family lives, shops, and cares for our health.

My Story

From searching for answers to helping others.

Over the last 13 years, God has guided me through learning how to create a lower-tox home, make more intentional choices, and support companies that align with my values. What started as a personal transformation has become a passion to help other women and families feel supported — not through fear or perfection, but through education, encouragement, and simple intentional steps.

This journey taught me that healthy living isn't about eliminating every possible risk. It's about becoming informed, making intentional choices, and finding realistic ways to care for ourselves and our families.

Motherhood & Values

Supporting moms. Serving families. Creating change.

Through my work in wellness, beauty, foster care, writing, and mentoring women, my heart is to help you discover better options for your family and find products and companies you can feel good about supporting.

I believe small, intentional choices can create a lasting impact — not only for our families today, but for generations to come.

Motherhood and wellness go hand in hand — and I'm so glad you're here as I enter a new chapter with Ringana.

From Awareness to Action

My journey to cleaner living.

01

A mainstream start

When I became a first-time mom in 2009, conversations around clean living, ingredients, and intentional consumer choices weren't part of everyday life. I used conventional cleaners and personal care, bought inexpensive makeup and skincare, and never thought much about the companies behind what I purchased.

02

The turning point

After the birth of my second child, a lingering illness became a turning point. I knew deep down I wanted a different approach — and through God's guidance, the right resources, and years of learning, I began transforming the way my family ate, lived, and shopped.

03

From personal change to purpose

My passion for wellness grew into educating and mentoring others. I've spent more than 20 years in graphic design, more than a decade in beauty and wellness, and since 2019 I've built a successful clean beauty business — mentoring women to build businesses that honor their families, their time, and their personal goals.

04

Supporting companies that align

I discovered how important it is to support companies committed to quality, transparency, and integrity — products we feel good about bringing into our homes. That conviction is what drew me to Ringana: fresh, toxin-free products from a family-founded company that has held the same standard for three decades.

05

A new chapter with Ringana

I'm excited to partner with Ringana and share products I can feel good about recommending to family and friends. Follow along as I test, learn, and help families create healthier homes — without fear, and without pretending perfection is the goal.
