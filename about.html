My Story

From chiropractic school to a conscious home.

I graciously fell into learning about leading a conscious lifestyle when I attended chiropractic school with my husband. We did not grow up health-conscious at all. Once we realized that everything we put in, on, and around our bodies affects our health, we were on a mission to find the safest products we can find.

This came with great disappointment, of course. Either products had clean labels and performed very poorly, or products performed beautifully only for us to find out the ingredients were not as clean as we expected. We're very selective in what we invite into our home with our children and what we recommend to others.
